Ink & Strength

The design language recovered from the prebuilt CSS. Its central idea: no border or surface is a fixed color. Every one is composed from an ink and a strength, so a single variable re-themes the entire product. Both controls below are live — this page is rendered by the same tokens it documents.

The label texture

Mono, uppercase, .12em tracking, 9–10px — set against normally-tracked sans body copy. This single contrast does most of the stylistic work in the product.

05 — Motion

One curve, four durations

08 — Before you rebuild

The light-first trap

The upstream system is light-first. Its .btn-primary is a black button on a light page. The static site runs dark and inverts it — and because the prebuilt chunk sets padding-block:0 !important inside @layer components, an unlayered !important override loses to it regardless of importance.

That is the entire origin of the duplicated override block sitting at the top of all four HTML files. In a React rebuild the problem doesn't exist, because you generate the CSS yourself — so author the buttons dark-first against the semantic tokens and leave the old block behind.

09 — Making it yours

What to keep, what to change

keep

The chassis

Ink-and-strength borders, the alpha ladder, the mono-uppercase label texture, the signature curve, warm-paper light mode. This is well-built neutral structure and it carries almost no brand identity of its own.

change

The two tells

The #00d294 accent and the Geist Pixel display face. Between them they carry nearly all of the recognisable identity — swap both and the result reads as yours rather than as a clone.
