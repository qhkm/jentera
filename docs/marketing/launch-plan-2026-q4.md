# Jentera Launch & Content Plan — Q4 2026

> Owner: qhkm dev · Last updated: 2026-09-07 · Working doc for the weekly content cron.

## Positioning north star

- **Workers:** "Hire an AI worker for your business. You run the business, Jentera runs the busywork."
- **Hook:** "Grok gives AI a computer. Jentera gives AI access to your business."
- **Target:** non-technical MY/SEA SME owners (F&B, retail, e-commerce). Speak outcomes, never agents/tools.
- **Language:** English + Bahasa Malaysia mix; BM variants on FB/TikTok.

## Current state (Sep 2026)

Live: jentera.ai landing + onboarding, Telegram bot + sprite runtime, approvals, activity record.
Not built: pricing page, payments, waitlist capture — these are Phase 0 blockers.

1. **Proof** — "What jentera did for wagyu.my this week" (screenshots + numbers)
