How it works

You know your business. AISAR learns it.

Instead of "here's a box of Lego, build something" — you just tell AISAR about your company, and it figures out what to build.

01 — Understand

Tell us about your business.

Drop your website, or answer a few questions. AISAR builds your Business Profile — team, customers, channels, systems, pain points.

02 — Recommend

We find where AI helps most.

AISAR maps your business functions to AI opportunities — ranked by impact, with estimated hours saved. You pick, or let it decide.

03 — Set up

It builds & connects everything.

WhatsApp, calendar, menu, inventory — AISAR connects each one and explains why. No integrations marketplace with 200 logos.

04 — Improve

It never stops finding more.

"You manually export reservations to Sheets every Friday — I can automate that. Want me to?" Your business keeps moving toward 80% AI.
